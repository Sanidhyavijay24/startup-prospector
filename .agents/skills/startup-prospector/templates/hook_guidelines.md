# Outreach Hook Guidelines & Grounding Rubric

Every outreach hook generated by the assistant must sound authentic, human, and direct (peer-to-peer or founder-to-founder).

---

## 1. The 3-Line Structure

Each hook must strictly consist of **2 to 3 sentences** (between 35 and 85 words):

* **Line 1 (The Trigger):** A specific, verifiable recent milestone (a named feature launch, changelog release, funding milestone, or public announcement).
* **Line 2 (The Product Observation):** A genuine observation highlighting what makes their implementation stand out or how it solves practical user friction.
* **Line 3 (The Value Prop Bridge):** A natural transition anchored to the selected campaign intent.

---

## 2. Hook A vs. Hook B Framework

Always synthesize two distinct angles for each prospective startup:

### Hook A: Trigger & Product Launch Angle
* **Anchor:** Newest product release, v2/v3 update, or major changelog entry.
* **Tone:** Fast, punchy, timely.
* **Best Channels:** LinkedIn DMs, X (Twitter) DMs.
* **Example:**
  > *"Read your post from earlier this month introducing the new terminal diff engine. Giving engineers git-native review controls without forcing another heavy IDE extension solves the exact friction developers complain about. We are putting together an AI developer tooling benchmark and want to feature DevFlow AI."*

### Hook B: Workflow & Technical Insight Angle
* **Anchor:** User experience, technical architecture, competitive differentiation, or founder-to-founder integration.
* **Tone:** Analytical, workflow-focused, thoughtful.
* **Best Channels:** Cold Email, Discord founder DMs, LinkedIn connection notes.
* **Example 1 (Reviewer / Benchmark Bridge):**
  > *"DevFlow AI has stood out for keeping the CLI interaction instantaneous while other platforms overload their sidebars with complicated settings. For everyday refactoring, a lightweight terminal workflow beats enterprise GUI tools every time. Would love to feature DevFlow AI in our upcoming developer tools guide."*
* **Example 2 (Founder-to-Founder Co-Marketing Bridge):**
  > *"DevFlow AI has stood out for keeping terminal diff interactions instantaneous without forcing an IDE reload. We are building TraceCLI to capture local debug sessions and noticed developers frequently pair git diffs with our traces. Would love to connect and explore a lightweight terminal integration."*

---

## 3. Strict Quality Gate (Pass vs. Fail)

### Prohibited Patterns:
* **NO Em Dashes:** Never use `—` or `--`. Use periods or commas.
* **NO AI Clichés:** Never use *"game changer"*, *"delve"*, *"testament"*, *"cutting-edge"*, *"revolutionary"*, *"spearhead"*, *"unleash"*, or *"elevate"*.
* **NO Generic Spam Openers:** Never start with *"I hope this email finds you well"*, *"I came across your profile"*, or *"In today's fast-paced world"*.

### Before vs. After Examples:

❌ **FAIL (AI Template Tone):**
> *"I hope this email finds you well! Your cutting-edge AI diff engine is a true game changer—a testament to your team's revolutionary work. Let's delve into how we can collaborate."*
*(Fails: Generic opener, cutting-edge, em dash, game changer, testament, delve).*

✅ **PASS (Authentic Founder Peer Tone):**
> *"Read your post from earlier this month introducing the new terminal diff engine. Giving engineers git-native review controls without forcing another heavy IDE extension solves the exact friction developers complain about. We are putting together an AI developer tooling benchmark and want to feature DevFlow AI."*
